# Preserve Raw Evidence Before Canonicalization

Every acquired record must retain its source evidence and provenance separately from the canonical business facts derived from it. We reject writing page-shaped fields directly into the durable business model because source formats can change and any current view must be traceable back to its origin; normalization may be revised without rewriting the evidence. See the Foundation layers and Slice 1 acceptance in [#10](https://github.com/linhaicue/nice-salon/issues/10).
